    Help Center

    Frequently Asked

    How do I place an order?

    Browse our collections, fill out the contact form, or message us on WhatsApp at +91 7506507833. We'll confirm availability, size, and delivery details with you directly.

    What is your return policy?

    We offer a 7-day return policy on unworn items with original tags. Contact us within 7 days of delivery to initiate an exchange or return.

    How long does delivery take?

    Orders are typically dispatched within 1–2 business days. Delivery across India usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.

    Do you offer custom sizing?

    Yes! For select ethnic and formal pieces, we can accommodate custom measurements. Mention your requirements in the order form or on WhatsApp.

    Is cash on delivery available?

    COD is available in select cities. Confirm with our team on WhatsApp when placing your order.
